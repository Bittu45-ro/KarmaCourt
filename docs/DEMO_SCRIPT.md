# KarmaCourt: 2-Minute Video Demo Script (Detective Edition)
> Target Duration: Exactly 100 - 120 seconds.
> Theme: Ace Attorney meets Reddit's favorite daily dilemmas.

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] The Hook & Problem
- **Visual:** Open on the Reddit home feed scrolling past r/AmItheAsshole.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Reddit loves drama and moral dilemmas. But right now, following these cases means digging through 500-word text posts and conflicting comments. What if you could step into the courtroom as a daily juror, inspect the evidence, and crack the case in 60 seconds? Meet **KarmaCourt: The Daily Reddit Trial**."

---

### [0:25 - 0:50] The Investigation Phase (The Evidence Locker)
- **Visual:** Click on the interactive KarmaCourt post. The courtroom loads with today's allegation: *Case #1042: The Carolina Reaper Lunch Trap*.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Every 24 hours, a new trial drops. Today, an employee is accused of sending an executive to the ER with ghost pepper sauce. 
  > Before I vote, I need evidence. I tap **Exhibit A** — the lunchbox was labeled 'PRIVATE PROPERTY.' 
  > I tap **Exhibit B** — HR emails proving management refused to stop lunch thieves.
  > And **Exhibit C** reveals the smoking gun: the accused has a certified history of eating ultra-spicy hot sauce at home."
- **Visual:** Click Exhibit A, B, and C. The buttons turn green with checkmarks (`✅ Exhibit A`).

---

### [0:50 - 1:15] The Verdict Gavel & Accord
- **Visual:** The user taps the green **NTA (Acquitted)** button. The Accord banner flashes green.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "With all three exhibits reviewed, I deliver my verdict: **NTA**.
  > Instantly, the gavel drops: Accord Reached! 74% of the community agreed with my judgment. 
  > KarmaCourt gives me the consensus legal rationale: you cannot be held liable for food safety when someone unlawfully steals labeled property from the company fridge!"

---

### [1:15 - 1:45] The Viral Comment Loop
- **Visual:** Click **'Copy Detective Scorecard to Comments'** and paste the Wordle-style text in the comment box.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "I tap Copy Scorecard and paste it straight into the Reddit comments: 3/3 Evidence Reviewed, Accord Reached, and a 2-Day Streak! It sparks immediate debate with other jurors right on the thread."

---

### [1:45 - 2:00] Technical Architecture & Wrap-Up
- **Visual:** Quick display of the GitHub repo with passing CI badges and Devvit architecture.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Built natively on the Reddit Developer Platform using Devvit JSX, TypeScript, and Redis KV for atomic vote counting and streak persistence.
  > Thank you for judging KarmaCourt!"
